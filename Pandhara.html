Pandhara Kuda

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Holarrhena pubescens (Wall. ex G.Don)

Common Name: Kurchi / Kuda / White Kuda

Family: Apocynaceae

Description

Pandhara Kuda is a shrub with white flowers and elongated green leaves. It is commonly found in India.

Medical Uses

  1. Traditionally used for diarrhoea and dysentery.
  2. Used in traditional medicine for intestinal disorders.
  3. Bark preparations are traditionally used for digestive problems.
